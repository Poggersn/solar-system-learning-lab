Form 2 Science: Solar System Learning Lab

Observe • Identify • Sequence • Explain

Learning challenge

Scenario: Your class is preparing a mini-exhibition about the Solar System. Use the infographic to answer the questions and explain why the order of the planets matters.

The diagram is schematic and not to scale. Planet descriptions are simplified for Form 2 revision.

Part A — Check your understanding

1. What is at the centre of our Solar System?

2. Which planet is third from the Sun?

3. Which planet is the largest in our Solar System?

4. Which planet is known for its prominent ring system?

5. Which sequence correctly lists the planets from the Sun outward?

Part B — Explain in your own words

Choose one planet and explain its position in the Solar System. Then state one fact about it from the infographic.

Use this as an exit ticket or pair discussion. Your teacher can assess the explanation separately.

Teacher check

Objective 1
Can the learner identify the Sun and name at least six planets in the correct order?
Objective 2
Can the learner answer at least four quiz questions correctly and explain one planet fact?
